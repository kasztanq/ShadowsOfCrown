DEVELOPER BLUEPRINT

How the 4-in-1 Chestnut Arcade Hub is Built Step-by-Step

STEP 1 4-Game State Machine & Shared Vector Renderer

A single `requestAnimationFrame(masterGameLoop)` manages all 4 cartridges via `currentMode = 'MENU' | 'SNAKE' | 'SPACE' | 'TANKS' | 'BOMBER'`. All games share the procedural `drawChestnut(ctx, x, y, radius, rotation, showBurr, isGolden)` renderer so zero external image files are required. 

if (currentMode === 'SNAKE') { updateSnakeGame(timestamp); drawSnakeGame(); }
else if (currentMode === 'SPACE') { updateSpaceGame(); drawSpaceGame(); }
else if (currentMode === 'TANKS') { updateTanksGame(); drawTanksGame(); }
else if (currentMode === 'BOMBER') { updateBomberGame(); drawBomberGame(); }

STEP 2 Cartridges #01 & #02: Chestnut Snake & Space Chestnut

Chestnut Snake uses a 32×20 grid with a directional input queue (`snake.dirQueue`) and spawns Golden Bonus Chestnuts every 5th pickup while scaling movement speed.

Space Chestnut implements a parallax starfield, spinning chestnut projectiles (`bullet.angle += 0.28`), wave-based enemy behaviors (sine-wave Acorn Bugs, rotating Burr-Asteroids, homing Space Squirrels), triple spread-shot upgrades, and the multi-phase Cosmic Burr Boss.

STEP 3 Cartridge #03: Chestnut Tanks (Battle City Arena, Levels & Mega-Tank Boss)

Chestnut Tanks divides the 800×500 arena into a 32×20 tactical grid (`25px` tiles) with destructible wooden brick walls (`tile === 1`), indestructible stone blocks (`tile === 2`), and the Golden Chestnut Base (`tile === 9`) at the bottom center.

  • Level 1: Scout & Light Acorn Tanks patrol the lanes and fire at brick walls and your base.
  • Level 2: Fast Raider Tanks & Heavy 2-Hit Armored Tanks aggressively flank the Golden Chestnut Base.
  • Level 3+ (Boss Stages & Endless Scaling): The massive Iron Husk Mega-Tank Boss rolls onto the battlefield with a Boss HP bar, 3-way chestnut spread cannons, and minion tank support!
  • Power-Ups: Collect Burr Shields (invulnerability), Rapid Nut Cannons (fast fire), and Base Repair kits (+1 hull & rebuilds base walls).

STEP 4 Cartridge #04: Bomber-Nut (Chestnut Bomberman, Raycast Blasts & King Burr-Golem)

Bomber-Nut uses a 17×11 maze grid (`44px` tiles) with indestructible stone pillars at even intersections and destructible autumn leaf/wood crates. Pressing `SPACEBAR` plants a ticking Spiky Chestnut Bomb that raycasts in 4 directions (`UP, DOWN, LEFT, RIGHT`) up to `player.blastRange` tiles, triggering chain reactions with other bombs!

  • Power-Ups in Crates: Extra Chestnut Bomb (`+1 Bomb`), Burr Flame Range (`+1 Range`), and Speed Boots (`+Speed`).
  • Level 1 & 2: Clear patrolling Forest Bugs (Level 1) and smart Chaser Beetles (Level 2) to unlock the glowing Autumn Exit Portal.
  • Level 3+ (Boss Arena): Face the King Burr-Golem Boss—a giant multi-tile boss that hurls spiky mini-burrs across the maze and must be tactically trapped with Chestnut Bombs!
