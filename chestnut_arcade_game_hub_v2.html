DEVELOPER BLUEPRINT

How the 4-in-1 Chestnut Arcade Hub is Built Step-by-Step

STEP 1 4-Game State Machine & Shared Vector Renderer

A single `requestAnimationFrame(masterGameLoop)` manages all 4 cartridges via `currentMode = 'MENU' | 'SNAKE' | 'SPACE' | 'TANKS' | 'BOMBER'`. All games share the procedural `drawChestnut(ctx, x, y, radius, rotation, showBurr, isGolden)` renderer so zero external image files are required. 

if (currentMode === 'SNAKE') { updateSnakeGame(timestamp); drawSnakeGame(); }
else if (currentMode === 'SPACE') { updateSpaceGame(); drawSpaceGame(); }
else if (currentMode === 'TANKS') { updateTanksGame(); drawTanksGame(); }
else if (currentMode === 'BOMBER') { updateBomberGame(); drawBomberGame(); }

STEP 2 Cartridges #01 & #02: Chestnut Snake & Overhauled Space Chestnut

Chestnut Snake uses a 32×20 grid with a directional input queue (`snake.dirQueue`) and spawns Golden Bonus Chestnuts every 5th pickup while scaling movement speed.

Space Chestnut uses a structured Sector & Sub-Wave State Machine (`Wave X-1`, `Wave X-2`, `Wave X-3`, followed by `Wave X-BOSS`) with a 2.5-second breather banner between every wave so bosses never spawn back-to-back:

  • 4 Distinct Sectors & Enemy Rosters: Deep Autumn Orbit (Bug Scouts, Burr-Asteroids, Space Squirrels, Acorn Fighters), Crimson Sap Nebula (Diagonal Leaf-Vipers, Splitting Cluster-Burrs, 3-Hit Bark-Cruisers), Golden Harvest Belt (Stealth Wasps & Sniper Turret Pods), and Omega Void.
  • 4 Unique End-of-Sector Bosses: Sector 1 Cosmic Burr-Titan (spread shots + drifting burr-mines), Sector 2 Mecha-Squirrel Dreadnought (lane dashes + twin lasers + homing Squirrel Drones), Sector 3 Queen Autumn Moth (figure-8 flight + crescent blades + telegraphed Solar Sap Beam), and Sector 4+ Omega Chestnut Core (3 orbiting Husk Shield Nodes + spiral bullet rings).
  • 4 Power-Up Capsules: Collect [WPN+] (Single → Twin → Triple → Quad + Homing Micro-Nuts), [SHD] (Spiky Burr Shield), [+1 HP] (Hull Repair), and [NOVA] (Super Chestnut Nova Bomb via E / B).

STEP 3 Cartridge #03: Chestnut Tanks (6 Biomes, Safe Base Perimeter & Mega-Tank Boss)

Chestnut Tanks uses a 21×13 tactical arena (`38px` tiles) with a forgiving `26x26` tank hitbox and automatic corner-sliding. The Golden Chestnut Base sits at `(baseCol = 10, baseRow = 12)` protected by 5 U-shaped shield bricks, while a guaranteed clear safety perimeter (`rows 10..12`, `cols 6..14`) ensures the tank is never boxed in on the left or right side of the base.

  • 6 Unique Level Biomes & Blueprints: Lv.1 Autumn Woodland Glade (Twin-Corridor Fortress), Lv.2 Crimson Canyon Quarry (Chevron Stone Bunkers), Lv.3 Iron Husk Citadel (Boss Arena), Lv.4 Frost-Pine Tundra (Cross-Bastion Maze), Lv.5 Golden Amber Stronghold (Hourglass Citadel), and Lv.6+ Volcanic Ember Forge.
  • Power-Ups & Safe Ejection: Collect Burr Shields (`SHD`), Rapid Nut Cannons (`SPD`), and Base Fortify kits (`FIX` — rebuilds the 5 U-shaped base bricks and automatically nudges any overlapping tank into the clear corridor).

STEP 4 Cartridge #04: Bomber-Nut (6 Stage Biomes, Custom Pillar Layouts & King Burr-Golem)

Bomber-Nut uses a 17×11 arena (`44px` tiles) that changes both its indestructible pillar architecture and background biome on every stage while keeping the player spawn corner `(1,1)`, `(1,2)`, and `(2,1)` 100% clear.

  • 6 Stage Biomes & Layouts: Stg.1 Mossy Forest Clearing (Classic Checkerboard), Stg.2 Twilight Mushroom Cavern (Cross Avenue & Corner Monoliths), Stg.3 King Burr-Golem's Amber Throne (Boss Arena Ring), Stg.4 Frosted Birch Ruins (Staggered Bastion Gates), Stg.5 Sunken Root Labyrinth (Concentric Chambers), and Stg.6+ Volcanic Cinder Pit.
  • Power-Ups & Bosses: Blast crates for `+1 Bomb`, `+1 Range`, and `Speed Boots`, unlock the glowing Exit Portal, and trap King Burr-Golem on every 3rd stage!
